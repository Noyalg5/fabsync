# Data dictionary: KPIs

**All data behind these KPIs is synthetic.** It represents no real company, supplier, customer or job.

Generated by `make kpi` from `config/kpis.yaml`. Each KPI is a documented SQL view in the `marts` schema;
`marts.kpi_definition` holds these records and `marts.kpi_scorecard` the current values. The caveat is
part of the definition: a KPI shown without its limitations is how dashboards lose trust.

## KPI definitions

| ID | KPI | Definition | Formula | Sources and systems | Owner | Refresh | Target | Caveat |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| KPI-01 | OTIF delivery performance | The share of deliveries that reached the customer by the promised date with the full tonnage on the lorry. | deliveries despatched on or before the promised date AND with delivery-note tonnage at least 98% of the works order weight, divided by deliveries with a promised date, x 100 | shop_floor.delivery_notes (despatch_date, promised_date, tonnage, job); corvus_mrp.works_orders (actual_finish); corvus_mrp.bom_lines with the material golden record (works order weight); finance.sales_invoices (customer name) | Production Controller | Daily, as delivery notes are entered | >= 95% | Measures despatch from our yard, not arrival on site, so haulage delays are invisible. Delivery notes do not name the works order, so "in full" relies on matching each note to the works order on the same job that finished just before it, which can pair wrongly when two finish on the same day. Delivery notes with no promised date (about 4%) are left out, and so are notes quarantined as unreadable, so the true figure could be worse. |
| KPI-02 | Labour variance | How many more (or fewer) hours the shop floor actually spent on completed work than the plan allowed. | (hours booked on completed works orders - planned hours on those works orders) / planned hours x 100 | corvus_mrp.works_orders (planned_hours, status); shop_floor.time_bookings (hours, works_order) | Production Controller | Weekly, after booking sheets are entered | within ±10% | Only as good as the booking sheets. Hours booked to a wrong or non-existent works order are left out (the job cost reconciliation reports how many), and unreadable or duplicate bookings are quarantined, so actual hours are understated. Planned hours are the estimator's figure, so a large variance can mean a poor estimate rather than poor performance. Works orders still in progress are excluded from the headline. |
| KPI-03 | Material yield and offcut waste | Of the steel bars we cut, the share that ends up in finished parts, and the share left over as offcut. | cut yield = metres of cut pieces / metres of 12 m stock bars needed to cut them x 100, weighted by kg and grouped by section type; offcut waste = 100 - cut yield | corvus_mrp.bom_lines (length_mm, qty, uom); material golden record (section type, kg per metre); corvus_mrp.goods_received (received quantity, for the received-to-issued ratio) | Production Controller | Monthly | >= 85% | A theoretical yield. It assumes each BOM line is cut from its own bars, so real nesting across lines would do better, and it assumes offcuts are scrapped, although some are reused. It does not cover plate, because there is no profile nesting data. The received-to-issued ratio mixes waste with stock building up in the yard, so it is not a waste figure on its own. |
| KPI-04 | Stock accuracy | The share of stock lines where what we counted in the yard matches what the system says we have. | stock lines whose counted quantity equals the book quantity / stock lines counted x 100, against a 95% target | corvus_mrp.stock (qty_on_hand, counted_qty, unit_cost); material golden record (section type) | Production Controller | After each cycle count; reported monthly | >= 95% | Counts every line equally, so a miscounted box of washers weighs the same as a missing rack of beams; the value error beside it shows which matters. It only covers lines that have been counted, and about a third were last counted more than 90 days ago. A count that agrees with a wrong book figure would pass. |
| KPI-05 | Three-way match exception rate | The share of steel purchase orders that do not match cleanly across order, delivery and invoice, and the money tied up in all purchasing exceptions. | PO lines with a quantity or price variance, missing GRN or missing invoice / PO lines assessed x 100; value at risk = sum of value at risk on those lines plus invoices with no PO | corvus_mrp.purchase_orders (qty, unit_price, promised_date); corvus_mrp.goods_received (qty_received, received_date); finance.purchase_invoices (net_amount, po_reference, nominal_code) | Finance Manager | Weekly, and at month end before the accruals are posted | <= 5% | Value at risk is not money lost. Most of it is subcontract and service spend with no purchase order, which is a control weakness but may be correctly priced; that spend is in the value but not the rate, because it has no order to match against. A 2% quantity tolerance flags every short delivery, even where the shortfall was agreed with the stockholder. Corvus orders carry one line each, so a partial delivery against a multi-line order would not show here. Orders not yet due for receipt or invoice are excluded until they are. |
| KPI-06 | Material traceability coverage | The share of steel used on our works orders that we can trace back to the mill certificate and cast it came from. | kg of BOM material on started works orders whose supplying receipts all carry a heat number and mill certificate and state the grade / total kg x 100 | corvus_mrp.goods_received (heat_number, mill_cert_ref, material_code, received_date); corvus_mrp.bom_lines; corvus_mrp.works_orders; shop_floor.delivery_notes | Quality Manager | Daily | >= 100% | Corvus records no material issues, so which delivery went into which works order is inferred by allocating deliveries first-in first-out. A real audit would need the cutting lists. A chain that shows as complete here has the right references on file; that does not prove the certificates were checked or the steel physically marked. The target is 100%, because EN 1090 allows no gaps. |
| KPI-07 | WIP value and ageing | The value of work started on the shop floor but not yet delivered, and how long it has been sitting there. | for works orders started and not cancelled, closed or despatched, BOM material value + hours booked to date x standard rate; aged from the first booking (or planned start) into 0-30, 31-60, 61-90 and 90+ day buckets | corvus_mrp.works_orders (status, planned_start); corvus_mrp.bom_lines with material prices from corvus_mrp.purchase_orders; shop_floor.time_bookings (hours, booking_date); shop_floor.delivery_notes | Finance Manager | Monthly, at period end | no target | Material is counted in full from the start of the works order, because Corvus does not record when steel is issued, so early-stage WIP is overstated. Labour is at a standard rate with no overhead. Works orders left open after the work has gone count as WIP until someone closes them, and many are, so the 90+ bucket is partly an admin backlog rather than stuck work. |
| KPI-08 | Capacity utilisation | The share of the hours our people were available in a week that were booked to jobs. | booked hours / available hours x 100, by week and site, as reported on the supervisors' weekly capacity sheet | shop_floor.weekly_capacity (available_hours, booked_hours, site, week_commencing); shop_floor.time_bookings (hours, as a cross-check) | Production Controller | Weekly | 75% to 95% | Uses the supervisors' own weekly totals, which drift a few per cent from the individual booking sheets (the cross-check column shows by how much). High utilisation is not the same as productive utilisation, because hours spent on rework count as booked. Weeks with no booked figure are left out. |
| KPI-09 | NCR rate and cost of quality | How often something we make goes wrong, and what putting it right costs as a share of what we sell. | cost of quality = sum of NCR cost impact / sales turnover x 100; NCR rate = NCRs raised per 100 tonnes despatched | shop_floor.ncr_log (raised_date, cost_impact, category); finance.sales_invoices (net_amount, invoice_date); shop_floor.delivery_notes (tonnage) | Quality Manager | Monthly | <= 2% | Covers only failure costs recorded on NCRs, not prevention or inspection, so the true cost of quality is higher. About a fifth of NCRs have no cost entered and count as nil. Turnover follows invoice timing, including applications for payment, so a single month's percentage can swing. Problems found and fixed without an NCR are invisible. |

## Views

Every object in the `marts` schema, with the description stored against it in the warehouse.

| View | KPI | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `marts.otif_by_customer` | KPI-01 | OTIF, on-time and in-full percentages by customer, worst first. |
| `marts.otif_by_month` | KPI-01 | OTIF, on-time and in-full percentages by month of despatch. |
| `marts.otif_by_site` | KPI-01 | OTIF, on-time and in-full percentages by fabrication site. |
| `marts.otif_deliveries` | KPI-01 | One row per delivery note with on-time, in-full and OTIF flags and the works order it is matched to. |
| `marts.labour_variance_by_job` | KPI-02 | Planned against booked hours on completed works orders, by job, largest variance first. |
| `marts.labour_variance_by_works_order` | KPI-02 | Planned against booked hours for every started works order. |
| `marts.material_yield_by_section_type` | KPI-03 | Cut yield, offcut waste and received-to-issued ratio by section type. |
| `marts.material_yield_lines` | KPI-03 | Cut yield for every BOM line of sections bought in stock bars. |
| `marts.stock_accuracy` | KPI-04 | Stock line accuracy and value error by site and for all sites, against target. |
| `marts.three_way_exception_rate` | KPI-05 | Exception rate and value at risk by month. |
| `marts.three_way_value_at_risk` | KPI-05 | Lines, value at risk and items over 90 days old by match category. |
| `marts.traceability_coverage` | KPI-06 | Traceability coverage by month and site, with despatched lines that are not traced. |
| `marts.wip_ageing` | KPI-07 | WIP value and count by age bucket. |
| `marts.wip_works_orders` | KPI-07 | Every works order in WIP with its value, age and bucket. |
| `marts.capacity_utilisation` | KPI-08 | Booked against available hours by week and site, with the booking-sheet cross-check. |
| `marts.capacity_utilisation_by_site` | KPI-08 | Utilisation by site over the whole period. |
| `marts.ncr_by_category` | KPI-09 | NCR count, cost and open NCRs by category. |
| `marts.quality_cost_by_month` | KPI-09 | NCRs, NCR cost, turnover, tonnes despatched, cost of quality and NCR rate by month. |
| `marts.bom_line_material` | helper | Every BOM line with its canonical material, section type, kilograms and value. |
| `marts.job_customer` | helper | Customer and site of each Corvus job, taking the customer name from finance invoices. |
| `marts.material_price` | helper | Average price paid per kilogram for each canonical material on Corvus purchase orders. |
| `marts.parameters` | helper | One-row table of the settings the KPI views use (as-of date, labour rate, bar length, targets). |
| `marts.works_order_despatch` | helper | The delivery note that despatched each finished works order, matched by job and date. |
| `marts.works_order_weight` | helper | Weight and material value of each works order, from its BOM. |
